# Documentation: Link, Don't Duplicate

Promoted to a skill: invoke `documentation-plugin:docs-single-source` when
writing or restructuring documentation — it carries the four principles
(link from where the reader starts, one canonical home per fact, a runnable
artifact is its own doc, reference the authoritative source), the five drift
smells and their fixes, when a one-line summary is a signpost rather than
duplication, and the "does this fact already live somewhere authoritative?"
litmus test.
